Kiln Shrink Sizer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

Kiln Shrink Sizer is a self-contained tool for potters. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • Everything you enter (clay bodies, shrinkage percentages, sizes) is stored only on your device.
  • The app has no accounts, no login, and no servers — nothing is sent off your device.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers.

Because no data ever leaves your device, there is no personal information for us to access, share, or sell.

Questions about this policy? Contact info@inveey.com.
