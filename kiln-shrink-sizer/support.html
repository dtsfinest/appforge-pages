Kiln Shrink Sizer — Support

Kiln Shrink Sizer helps potters hit an exact finished size by accounting for how clay shrinks from wet to fired.

How to use it

  • Pick your clay body — or add one with its measured shrinkage percentage.
  • Choose a direction: enter a finished size to get the wet size to throw, or enter a wet size to predict the finished size.
  • Manage your saved clay bodies on the Clay Bodies tab.
